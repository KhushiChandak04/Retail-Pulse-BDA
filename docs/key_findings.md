# RetailPulse Key Findings

The findings below use only values generated by the RetailPulse Spark SQL
pipeline. Monetary values are reported in the dataset's currency units.

## 1. Overall valid sales generated substantial revenue

- **Finding:** The project recorded strong overall valid-sales activity.
- **Exact metric/value:** Revenue was **20,972,594.57** across **40,077 orders** and **11,420,305 units**. The average order value was **523.31**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** All valid sales in the supplied Online Retail II dataset, from December 2009 through December 2011.
- **Business interpretation:** The dashboard's headline KPIs show the overall scale of sales and provide the baseline for comparing products, countries, and customer groups.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/kpi_summary.csv`

## 2. November 2011 was the highest-revenue month

- **Finding:** November 2011 produced the highest monthly revenue in the generated sales output.
- **Exact metric/value:** Revenue was **1,509,496.33** from **2,769 orders** and **754,507 units**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Valid sales in **2011-11**.
- **Business interpretation:** November was the strongest observed sales period and is a useful month for investigating seasonal demand, product mix, and customer activity.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/monthly_sales.csv`

## 3. The final December period is lower but incomplete

- **Finding:** December 2011 shows lower revenue than the surrounding peak months.
- **Exact metric/value:** December 2011 revenue was **638,810.68** from **819 orders** and **314,249 units**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Valid sales in **2011-12**.
- **Business interpretation:** The dataset ends on **2011-12-09**, so this is a partial month and should not be interpreted as a complete-month decline in performance.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/monthly_sales.csv`

## 4. Regency CakeStand 3 Tier was the top product by revenue

- **Finding:** `REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER` ranked first by product revenue.
- **Exact metric/value:** Stock code `22423` generated **344,563.25** from **3,918 orders** and **27,577 units**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** All valid sales grouped by product.
- **Business interpretation:** This product was the largest individual revenue contributor and should be considered in product promotion, inventory, and merchandising discussions.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/top_products.csv` and `outputs/product_sales.csv`

## 5. Jumbo Bag Red White Spot had the highest unit volume among the listed products

- **Finding:** `JUMBO BAG RED WHITE SPOT` recorded the highest unit volume in the product-sales output.
- **Exact metric/value:** Stock code `85099B` sold **98,349 units**, generated **183,454.83** in revenue, and appeared in **3,989 orders**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** All valid sales grouped by product.
- **Business interpretation:** High unit volume does not automatically mean highest revenue; this product demonstrates the difference between demand measured by units and value measured by revenue.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/product_sales.csv`

## 6. The United Kingdom dominated country-level revenue

- **Finding:** The United Kingdom was the leading country by a wide margin.
- **Exact metric/value:** United Kingdom revenue was **17,870,977.78** across **36,535 orders**, with **5,350 identifiable customers**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** All valid sales grouped by country.
- **Business interpretation:** The business was primarily dependent on its United Kingdom market, while other countries represent smaller international sales opportunities.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/country_sales.csv`

## 7. Customer spending was concentrated among a small number of high-value customers

- **Finding:** Customer analysis identified a clear highest-spending customer.
- **Exact metric/value:** Customer `18102` spent **608,821.65** across **145 orders**, **382 unique products**, and **188,340 units**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Valid sales with a known customer ID.
- **Business interpretation:** High-value customers can have a material effect on revenue, so retention and account-level service may be important for business performance.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/customer_analysis.csv`

## 8. Repeat and frequent customers formed the majority of identifiable customers

- **Finding:** Customers were distributed across all three project-defined segments.
- **Exact metric/value:** There were **2,454 Repeat Customers**, **1,801 Frequent Customers**, and **1,623 One-time Customers**, for **5,878 identifiable customers** in total.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Valid sales with known customer IDs, segmented by distinct order frequency.
- **Business interpretation:** Customers with more than one order formed the majority of the identifiable customer base, indicating a substantial opportunity for retention and repeat-purchase strategies.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/customer_segments.csv`

## 9. Frequent customers had much higher average spending than one-time customers

- **Finding:** The Frequent Customer segment had the highest average customer spend.
- **Exact metric/value:** Frequent Customers averaged **7,899.27** in spend and **15.39 orders**; One-time Customers averaged **350.08** in spend and **1.00 order**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Customer segments based on valid sales with known customer IDs.
- **Business interpretation:** Purchase frequency is strongly associated with customer value in this dataset, supporting loyalty, reactivation, and repeat-purchase analysis.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/customer_segments.csv`

## 10. Cancellation activity was highest by value in December 2011

- **Finding:** December 2011 had the highest cancellation value in the cancellation output.
- **Exact metric/value:** Cancellation value was **205,124.67**, covering **176 cancellation invoices** and **88,719 cancelled units**.
- **Relevant time period or population:** Cancellation rows in **2011-12**; the dataset covers only through December 9 for this month.
- **Business interpretation:** The December result should be monitored alongside the partial-month sales result because incomplete-period activity can distort comparisons.
- **Supporting output:** `outputs/cancellation_analysis.csv`

## Presentation Summary

The results show a large United Kingdom-led retail business, strong seasonal
activity around late 2011, concentration in a small group of high-value
products and customers, and a meaningful difference between one-time and
frequent customer value. Cancellation trends should be presented with the
dataset's partial December 2011 coverage clearly stated.
